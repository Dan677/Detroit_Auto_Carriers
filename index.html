All Services

Trusted For Our Services

Trusted by clients across the country, Detroit Auto Carriers delivers exceptional vehicle transport services with safety and reliability at the core. Our advanced systems and experienced team ensure smooth operations, while our dedication to professionalism guarantees that every car is handled with the utmost care. Whether you need individual deliveries or large-scale logistics solutions, we combine technology, expertise, and passion to exceed expectations and make every journey worry-free."

  • 01 Vehicle Transport

    When time is of the essence, the urgent shipping option ensures delivery is made as quickly as possible. Priority handling and a trusted carrier network guarantee both speed and security, so your shipment arrives without delay.

  • 02 Storage

    Our secure vehicle storage service offers peace of mind and flexibility. Whether you need short-term or long-term storage, your car will be kept in a safe and monitored facility until you’re ready for delivery. Skip the worry of limited space or unsafe parking — we provide a protected environment and handle all the logistics so you don’t have to.

  • 03 Care & Maintenance

    Our Care & Maintenance service keeps your vehicle in top condition while in our care. From regular check-ups to battery maintenance and tire monitoring, we ensure your car stays road-ready during storage or transit. It’s the ideal solution for long-term peace of mind and vehicle reliability.

  • 04 Title & Registration

    Title & Registration service takes the paperwork hassle off your hands. From handling title transfers to completing registration documents, the process is fast, accurate, and stress-free. The simplest way to keep your vehicle fully compliant and ready for the road.

  • 05 EV Logistics

    EV Logistics service is designed specifically for electric vehicles, ensuring safe and efficient transport every step of the way. From specialized loading and handling to secure battery management, everything is tailored to meet EV requirements. The perfect solution for moving electric cars with confidence and care.

  • 06 Vehicle Repossession

    Vehicle Repossession service provides fast, secure, and compliant recovery of vehicles from any location. With trained agents and reliable logistics, the process is handled efficiently and professionally. The ideal solution for lenders, leasing companies, or businesses needing trusted vehicle recovery.

  • 07 Home Delivery & Pickup

    Home Delivery & Pickup service brings maximum convenience by collecting your vehicle directly from your location and delivering it right to your doorstep. No need for terminals or extra trips — everything is handled smoothly, saving you time and effort.

Our Posts

Latest News & Posts

Stay informed with our latest articles, insights, and updates from the transport and logistics industry.

  • 01 Why Communication Is the Key to Stress-Free Car Shipping

    We’ve noticed that one of the biggest worries customers have is not knowing where their car is during transport. That’s why we focus so much on communication. From the moment a booking is confirmed, our team keeps customers updated about pickup times, carrier details, and estimated arrivals. Just last month, a customer told us that getting regular updates made the whole process “surprisingly easy.” That kind of feedback motivates us to keep raising the standard — because at the end of the day, shipping cars is about trust.

  • 02 What It’s Like Delivering a Classic Car to Its New Owner

    One of the best parts of this job is seeing the smile on a customer’s face when their dream car rolls off the carrier. Recently, we delivered a 1969 Mustang to a collector in Texas. It wasn’t just about the logistics — it was about treating the car like the rare treasure it is. We used enclosed transport, custom tie-downs, and double-checked every detail before it left. For us, it’s moments like these that remind us why we started Detroit Auto Carriers: every vehicle has a story, and we’re proud to be part of it.

  • 03 How We Handle Urgent Shipments When Every Hour Counts

    Sometimes, moving a vehicle isn’t just about convenience — it’s urgent. We once had a customer who needed their car delivered within two days for a job relocation. Our team coordinated with our trusted carrier network and prioritized the shipment. It wasn’t easy, but the car arrived on time and safe. Experiences like this push us to keep improving, because we know that behind every vehicle there’s a person relying on us.

  • 04 The Day We Moved a Fleet of Company Cars

    Not every job is about a single vehicle. Last year, a business trusted us to transport an entire fleet of company cars across three states. Coordinating that move meant working closely with their managers, making sure every driver knew exactly where to be, and double-checking schedules. It was a challenge, but seeing the relief on the client’s face when every car arrived together, on time and damage-free, made the effort worthwhile. Jobs like these remind us how important logistics and teamwork are in this industry.

  • 05 From Michigan to Texas: What Long-Haul Transport Teaches Us

    Every long route has its own challenges. One of the busiest lanes for us is Michigan to Texas. Weather shifts, road conditions, and tight schedules mean our drivers have to stay sharp every mile of the way. But it’s also one of the routes where we’ve built some of our best customer relationships. Whether it’s a family relocating or a dealer sending multiple cars south, we’ve learned that planning ahead and staying flexible is the secret to making every delivery on time.
